Why this domain?

  • A tabletop physics puzzle: the robot frees balloons tied to a box, and the box floats up to where the balloons' pull matches its weight.
  • The lift law is hidden: how hard each balloon colour pulls, how the pull fades with height, how heavy each box material is. On the visible base simulator an open clip frees nothing and the box never moves.
  • The answer to a level is a subset of clips to open, a small discrete choice, but which subset is right is a quantitative composition of per-colour lifts, the fade and the box's mass. Exactly one subset per level lands the box in the band.
  • One balloon too many and the box rises to the ceiling, where a balloon bursts and the level is lost. A freed balloon cannot be clipped back.
  • Contrast with busyboard, which hides discrete structure with no numbers to fit: here two or three training levels give a few hover heights, each an equation in the unknown constants, and a fit turns the test level into arithmetic.

The world

A test level at its start: four clipped balloons, one box, the green band above it.

  • A box at the left of the table; its colour names its material (pine, oak, teak).
  • A rack of balloons along the back, each tied by a string to the box and held in the rack by a clip in front of it. A balloon's colour is its gas.
  • A translucent green band beside the box's column: the heights the goal wants.
  • A ceiling plate 0.78 m above the table.
  • Goal. The box hangs at rest with its centre inside the band, and no balloon has burst.
  • Controls. Push a clip open (Release) or hold still (Wait). Nothing else.

  • Every feature above is observable; there is no partially observable variant. The hidden part is the law, not a feature: the lift of each colour, the fade height, the mass behind each box colour, the air drag.
  • color is an identity that carries physics: the same gas pulls the same on every level, and the same wood weighs the same, so a rule stated over colours transfers to a rack never seen.
  • Objects are named box, balloon0.., clip0.. (clip i holds balloon i), band, robot. The goal text names the box by material and gives the band's heights, e.g. "Open clips to free balloons so that the oak box floats up and hangs still with its centre inside the green band (0.77 to 0.82 m)".
  • Not in the state: the strings (a balloon's tie is the tied flag), the forces, the constants of the law.

Release, lift, hover, pop

  • Release. The freed balloon flies to the end of its string and seats on the box's top; later balloons stack above earlier ones.
  • Lift. Every intact freed balloon pulls the box up with a force set by its colour. The pull fades linearly with the box's height.
  • Hover. With enough balloons the box rises until the fading pull equals its weight, and hangs there. Air drag settles it.
  • Pop. A balloon that reaches the ceiling bursts and pulls no more. That loses the level, and a freed balloon cannot be clipped back.
  • On the visible base simulator a clip opens and nothing else happens. Everything above is absent from it.

A train level: three balloons, an oak box, band at 0.77 to 0.82 m.

Hidden mechanics live in pybullet_balloons.py; the base sim in pybullet_balloons_base.py is copied into the agent's sandbox as reference source.

The hidden law: two lines of physics

Lc(z) = Lc · (1 - (z - ztable) / h) lift of colour c on a box at height z; h is the fade height
z* = ztable + h · (1 - W / Σ Lc) the hover height, where W is the weight of the box plus its freed balloons

Predicates and skills

Env predicates

  • InBand(box, band): the box hangs at rest with its centre inside the band; the goal is this one atom
  • Tied(b), Untied(b): whether balloon b has been freed and pulled onto the box
  • Intact(b), Popped(b)
  • ClipOn(c), ClipOff(c): open or closed
  • AtRest(box): speed below the settle threshold; HandEmpty(robot)

Oracle-only helpers (ground_truth_models/balloons/)

  • Needed(b): the balloon is in the level's answer subset; Holds(clip, b); derived AllNeededTied(band)
  • Process Rise (the box climbs once every needed balloon is tied) and the endogenous Wait; the oracle opens the answer subset's clips weakest lift first.

Skills (shared push factory)

  • Release(robot, clip)[approach, contact_z]: push the clip's toggle open; the same push skill busyboard uses on its switches, with defaults balloons_push_approach 0.07 and balloons_push_contact_z 0.05
  • Wait(robot)[]: hold still while the physics plays out; the box needs a few hundred steps to rise and settle

Evaluator. The level is won when the goal atom holds and the box is at rest. A burst balloon terminates the episode and the certificate says which balloon burst on the ceiling.

Under the continual protocol the agent arms start with no predicates: the observation is the features above and a render, and the model arm invents its own predicates.

Train / test split: the contract

SplitBalloonsBox materialsHow the rack is drawn
Train2 or 3 (balloons_num_balloons_train)pine or oaka covering draw: each level prefers the colours and the material earlier train levels have not shown, so two levels together show every colour and both materials
Test4 (balloons_num_balloons_test): the whole palettepine or oaka rack training never showed, built from lifts and a mass it did
  • The band is built from the answer. For the drawn rack the generator lists every subset that lifts the box and its analytic hover height, keeps the heights that clear the ceiling with the stack of balloons and lie at least 6 cm from every other subset's height, draws one, and sets the band to that height plus or minus 2.5 cm (balloons_band_half).
  • Exactly one subset per level lands in the band by the law. The level is kept only if the oracle's own plan, open that subset's clips weakest lift first, hangs the box in the band on the simulator's physics with nothing burst (balloons_max_sampling_attempts 40 tries).
  • With four balloons there are 15 subsets; 14 of them either fail to lift the box or fly it past the band. In the test level on the next slide the pine box wants red + blue, at 0.646 m.
  • Train and test use separate random streams; a run under the continual protocol plays one training level (or two) and then one test level, and the test level allows no reset: one wrong clip loses it.

The split in practice

  • Training shows red, gold and blue on an oak box; the test rack adds green, on a lighter pine box. Nothing in the test level is new but the combination.
  • An agent that took "open everything" from training would fly the pine box into the ceiling with four balloons; an agent that identified the three lifts, the fade and the oak mass in training still needs the pine mass, which the other train level shows.

What has to be learned: structure first, then parameters

Structure: rules the base sim lacks

  • An open clip frees its balloon, which seats on the box (an attachment).
  • A freed balloon applies an upward force on the box that depends on its colour and on height.
  • A balloon at the ceiling pops and stops pulling.
  • The agent writes these as residual rules on the base sim, through the same command channel the true env uses (Attach, ApplyForce).

Parameters: constants to identify

  • Four per-colour lifts and one shared fade height.
  • The mass of each box material, both shown in training.
  • The air drag, which only affects how fast the box settles.
  • Two or three train levels give a few hover heights. Each is one equation in the lifts, the fade and the mass, so a fit pins the law and the test subset for a heavier box becomes arithmetic.

Why it favours a model: a guessing agent pays for each wrong subset with a level; a model-fitting agent computes the subset before touching a clip. This is the mirror image of busyboard, where the unknown is discrete and a parameter fit has nothing to grip.

Under the continual protocol

  • One run per seed plays a training level, then a test level; every low-level step is counted, the sandbox is free, and the pooled step cap is the only step budget (no episode horizon since 2026-09-06). Test levels have no resets.
  • Two arms: the model-based agent_continual, with run_python over the recorded data and sim.fit / sim.run on its own simulator.py, and the model-free agent_continual_model_free, with the same env and skill tools and no model.
  • The oracle plays both levels in docs/envs/balloons/oracle_solve.mp4; the frames in this deck are from it.
  • The launch config parks the env by default (SKIP: True in scripts/configs/predicatorv3/protocol_continual.yaml); the env flags live in envs/all.yaml (sesame_check_expected_atoms False, since the box reaches the band a few ticks after the last release).
  • Status: no agent run has been launched on this domain yet. The oracle solves its levels; the numbers to watch when the arms run are steps to each win, resets on the training level, and whether the test subset was computed or guessed.

Glossary

NameMeaning
lift, fade heightThe upward force a balloon colour exerts at table height, and the height over which it falls linearly to zero.

bandThe goal: a 5 cm range of heights set to one subset's hover height, plus or minus 2.5 cm.
